# SignLocal launch video brief

## Intent

Create a concise launch promo that proves how SignLocal works with the real product: open a PDF, add a signature, place it, and download the signed copy. The story should sell the product while showing the workflow clearly enough that a viewer understands it without sound.

## User-stated direction

- This is an X launch post for SignLocal.
- Make a video showing how the app works.
- The live product is free and ready to use.

## Inferred and defaulted decisions

- Square 1080x1080 because the destination is the X feed.
- Target 30 seconds: enough time to show the actual workflow without turning the launch into a tutorial.
- Automated build with no storyboard pause, based on Adam's direction to do it.
- No narration. Use short on-screen copy because X commonly autoplays muted.
- Feature real captured product screens instead of recreating a fake interface.
- Keep the visual system aligned with SignLocal: charcoal, warm ivory, teal, restrained amber.

## Required product beats

1. Hook: signing one PDF should not require another subscription.
2. Open a real disposable sample PDF in SignLocal.
3. Draw and place a signature on the document.
4. Download the signed copy.
5. Privacy proof: the file stays in the browser; no account or upload server.
6. Close on the live URL and "Free to use."

## Assets

- Capture the live SignLocal site and its real working states.
- Use only a disposable generated test PDF with no personal information.
- Existing launch artwork: `../../media/signlocal-x-launch.png`.
- Do not include the private SS-4, an SSN, home address, personal signature, or any other private business record.

## Customizations

- Silent video: no voiceover and no background music.
- Use readable text cards and subtle cursor/placement emphasis.
- No fake charts, testimonials, user counts, legal claims, or evidence-looking documents.
- End card must show `signlocal-adam-designs.netlify.app` exactly.

## Delivery

- Produce a verified preview, contact sheet, and final MP4 after Adam approves rendering.
- Update the X launch package with the final caption and video path; do not publish to X automatically.
