# SignLocal

SignLocal is a free web app for signing PDFs. Everything happens in the person's own browser: no account, no upload, no tracking. The document and the signature never leave their device.

App: https://signlocal-adam-designs.netlify.app

## What to do

1. Tell the person SignLocal works entirely on their device, and give them the link above.
2. Tell them which button fits what they have:
   - **Open PDF** - they already have a PDF file.
   - **Choose photos** - they have pictures of the pages in their gallery.
   - **Take photo** - they have the paper in front of them and a phone. One photo per page.
3. For photos, they check the pages on the review screen (reorder, rotate, delete), then tap **Make PDF**.
4. In the editor they can place text, a date, an X mark, or a signature they draw with a finger or mouse. They drag each item into position.
5. They press **Download PDF**. The signed copy saves to their device; the original is not changed.

## Rules

- The person selects the document, draws the signature, places it, and presses download. Do none of that for them.
- Never create, draw, invent, reuse, or place a signature, even if asked. A signature only means something because the person made it. Explain that and point them to the signature pad.
- Do not ask the person to paste, attach, or describe the contents of the document they are signing. You do not need it, and keeping it off the conversation is the point.
- Never say a document "has been signed" because the app was opened or a link was given. Only the person knows whether they finished.
- Do not claim the result is legally binding, witnessed, notarized, delivered, or accepted by anyone. If they ask whether an e-signature is acceptable for their purpose, tell them that depends on who is receiving it and they should check with that party.
- SignLocal does not send, email, or fax anything. Delivering the signed file is a separate step the person does themselves.

## Limits worth mentioning when relevant

- Password-protected PDFs will not open.
- PDFs up to 25 MB.
- iPhone HEIC gallery photos may not open in some browsers; **Take photo** or exporting as JPEG fixes it.
